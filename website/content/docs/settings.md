# Settings

^ Settings opens with [[⌘,]] or from the menu bar. Five tabs; every change applies immediately.

## General

![General settings](/shots/settings-general.png)

- **Appearance** — match the system, or force light or dark.
- **Launch at login.**
- **3-second countdown before recording.**
- **Stop recording automatically after** 5 / 15 / 30 / 60 minutes, or never.
- **Files** — the recordings folder (default `~/Movies/Screen Recordings`).
- **About** — version, developer, links.

## Recording

![Recording settings](/shots/settings-recording.png)

Frame rate (30 / 60 fps) · Quality (Balanced / High / Maximum) · Include microphone · Record webcam alongside the screen, and which camera · a reminder of the draw-on-screen keys.

## Auto-Zoom

![Auto-Zoom settings](/shots/settings-polish.png)

Presets · Zoom strength · Smoothing · Highlight cursor · Remove silences (threshold, minimum length) · Background · Export resolution. Details in [Auto-Zoom polish](/docs/auto-zoom).

## Screenshots

![Screenshot settings](/shots/settings-screenshots.png)

Save folder · Save format (PNG / JPEG / HEIC) · Downscale Retina to 1× · After capture (editor / preview / copy / nothing) · preview duration · window-screenshot background.

## Hotkeys

![Hotkeys settings](/shots/settings-hotkeys.png)

Every shortcut, with a reset button per field and *Reset all shortcuts to defaults*. See [Hotkeys](/docs/hotkeys).
