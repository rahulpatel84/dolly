# Screenshots

^ Capture a whole display, a dragged area, or a single window — at full native resolution — then edit, copy, save or share.

## Capture

| Capture | Shortcut | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| **Screen** | [[⌃⌘S]] | The display under the pointer. |
| **Area** | [[⌃⌘A]] | Every display dims; drag a rectangle on any of them. **Click** a window instead of dragging to capture exactly that window. [[Esc]] cancels. The size readout follows the pointer. |
| **Window** | [[⌃⌘W]] | Click a window. Its background is chosen in Settings → Screenshots: system shadow, transparent, solid colour, gradient, or trimmed. |

All three are also in the menu bar and the dashboard quick actions.

## Resolution and format

Screenshots are captured at the display's native pixel size (a Retina display gives 2× pixels). Turn on *Downscale Retina screenshots to 1×* only if you want smaller files. The default save format is lossless PNG; JPEG and HEIC are available in Settings → Screenshots → Saving. For a one-off format or compression level, use **Export** in the editor.

## After capture

![Screenshot settings](/shots/settings-screenshots.png "Settings → Screenshots.")

Settings → Screenshots → *After capture → Then*:

- **Open editor** (default) — the [editor](/docs/editor) appears immediately.
- **Show preview thumbnail** — a small thumbnail slides in at the bottom right for 4, 8 or 15 seconds. Click it to edit, drag it into another app, or let it fade.
- **Copy only** — copies to the clipboard and saves silently.
- **Nothing** — just saves.

The file is written in the background and copied to the clipboard as soon as it is ready, unless you copied something else in the meantime.
